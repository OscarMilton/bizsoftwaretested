Why this site exists

Search for accounting software and almost everything on the first page is written by someone selling it — the vendors themselves, or accountancy firms hoping you will hire them instead. The most useful answer is usually a forum thread where somebody who has actually used both products says what they think.

That is the gap this fills. I work in finance and administration for a manufacturing business, use Sage 50 Accounts every working day, and handle the bookkeeping, procurement and reporting that this software is supposed to make easier.

So when I compare two products, I have signed up for both, done real work in them, and hit the parts that do not work properly. Every review says how long I used it and what I disliked.
